# Privacy Policy — Market Ticker

**Market Ticker — Live Stocks, Crypto & Forex Prices** (Chrome extension)
Chrome Web Store ID: `bpbmlhbeamcacmafkifohcjmlpeiging`

*Last updated: 18 August 2026 · Applies from version 2.4.0*

---

## In short

Market Ticker does **not** track your browsing, does **not** read the pages you visit, and
contains no advertising or analytics trackers. Your settings stay on your own device.
Data is sent to a server in only two situations: to fetch the prices you asked for, and
when you deliberately submit feedback or a rating.

---

## 1. Information stored on your device

Saved locally using Chrome's storage; never transmitted anywhere:

- Your watchlist, and which symbols are pinned
- Your theme choice, and the widget's position and display state
- Whether you have hidden the widget
- Counters used to decide whether to show a rating prompt, and whether you have already
  rated or dismissed it

Uninstalling the extension removes all of it. You can also clear it at any time from
Chrome's extension settings.

## 2. Information sent when prices are fetched

To display prices, the extension sends **the ticker symbols on your watchlist** to two
third-party services:

| Service | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| TradingView | Your watchlist ticker symbols | To retrieve current price and daily change |
| Yahoo Finance | The text you type into symbol search | To find matching companies and tickers |

These requests contain no identifier for you — no account, no install ID, and no cookie set
by this extension. Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies.

## 3. Information sent when you submit feedback or a rating

This happens **only when you actively choose it** — tapping a star, or writing a message and
pressing send. Nothing is sent in the background. The data goes to a private Google Sheet
owned by the developer and is not shared with anyone else.

| Field | Example | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Star rating | `4` | To understand overall satisfaction |
| Your message | "BTC price stopped updating" | Only if you write one |
| Category | `missing-symbol` | To triage the report |
| **Email** | *optional* | **Only if you choose to type it**, so we can reply |
| Install ID | `8f3a…c21d` | A random identifier generated on install. Not linked to you, your Google account, or any other service |
| Extension version | `2.4.0` | To know which build a report refers to |
| Language | `en-IN` | To prioritise localisation |
| Operating system | `macOS` | To reproduce platform-specific bugs |
| Days installed | `12` | To distinguish new users from long-term ones |
| Times popup opened | `9` | To distinguish light from heavy usage |
| Watchlist size | `7` | A count only — **never the symbols themselves** |

## 4. What is never collected

- **Browsing history** — the extension does not record, store or transmit which sites you visit
- **Page content** — the widget renders in an isolated shadow DOM and does not read the page it appears on
- **Keystrokes, clicks or mouse movement** on web pages
- **Your identity** — no name, no account, no sign-in. The only personal detail ever collected
  is an email address, and only if you type one in yourself
- **Your watchlist contents** in feedback submissions — only the number of symbols
- **Cookies, advertising identifiers or third-party analytics** — the extension contains none

## 5. How the information is used

Feedback is used solely to fix bugs, decide what to build next, and reply to you if you asked
for a reply. It is **not sold**, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising,
profiling, or credit assessment.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Feedback entries are kept while they remain useful for product decisions. To have a submission
deleted, email the address below with the approximate date and, if you have it, the install ID.
Local settings can be cleared from Chrome's extension settings, and uninstalling removes all
local data immediately.

## 7. Children

Market Ticker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date. Continued use after a
